## 政策正文（英文，可直接交付）

> 以下为 Oak Shield 对外使用的政策文档，建议保留英文原文不翻译。

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                  OAK SHIELD SERVICE LLC
              DRUG-FREE WORKPLACE POLICY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Document ID:        OSS-HR-POL-001
Effective Date:     [INSERT DATE]
Revision:           1.0
Approved By:        Xinchao Xi, President
Policy Owner:       [INSERT POLICY ADMINISTRATOR NAME / TITLE]

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. PURPOSE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Oak Shield Service LLC ("Oak Shield" or "the Company") is committed
to providing a safe, healthy, and productive workplace for all
employees, subcontractors, visitors, and the public affected by
our operations. The use, possession, distribution, manufacture, or
sale of illegal drugs, controlled substances, or alcohol on Company
or client premises, in Company vehicles, or during work hours
creates an unacceptable risk to safety, quality, and Company
reputation.

This Policy establishes the Company's program to maintain a
drug-free workplace consistent with applicable federal, Texas, and
local law, and with the requirements of our clients, general
contractors, and project owners.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. SCOPE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

This Policy applies to:

  (a) All Oak Shield Service LLC employees, regardless of position
      or length of service;
  (b) All applicants for employment with the Company;
  (c) All temporary, seasonal, and contract workers performing
      work on behalf of the Company;
  (d) All subcontractors and their personnel while on Oak Shield
      project sites (through flow-down contract language); and
  (e) All Company-owned, leased, or rented vehicles and equipment.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. PROHIBITED CONDUCT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The following conduct is strictly prohibited and constitutes a
violation of this Policy:

  3.1  The unlawful manufacture, distribution, dispensation,
       possession, sale, purchase, transfer, or use of illegal
       drugs, controlled substances, drug paraphernalia, or
       alcohol on Company premises, client premises, project
       sites, or in Company vehicles.

  3.2  Reporting to work, or remaining at work, while under the
       influence of illegal drugs, controlled substances, alcohol,
       or any prescription or over-the-counter medication that
       impairs the employee's ability to perform job duties safely.

  3.3  Refusing to submit to a drug or alcohol test required under
       this Policy.

  3.4  Adulterating, substituting, or tampering with any specimen
       provided for testing.

  3.5  Failing to notify the Company within five (5) calendar days
       of any criminal drug statute conviction for a violation
       occurring in the workplace.

  3.6  Off-duty use of illegal drugs or controlled substances
       which results in impairment on the job, a positive test
       result, or which adversely affects job performance, safety,
       or the reputation of the Company.

Lawful use of prescription medication is permitted, provided the
employee is using the medication as prescribed and the medication
does not impair the employee's ability to perform the job safely.
Employees taking prescription medication that may affect safety
or performance must notify their supervisor before the start of
the work shift.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. TESTING PROGRAM
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

All testing under this Policy shall be performed by a qualified
SAMHSA-certified laboratory using a chain-of-custody process, with
results reviewed by a qualified Medical Review Officer (MRO).
The standard testing panel shall include, at minimum: marijuana
(THC), cocaine, opiates, amphetamines, and phencyclidine (PCP),
with alcohol testing where applicable.

  4.1  Pre-Employment Testing
       All applicants who have received a conditional offer of
       employment shall be required to pass a drug test prior to
       starting work. An applicant who tests positive, refuses to
       test, or attempts to tamper with the specimen shall have
       the conditional offer withdrawn.

  4.2  Random Testing
       Employees in safety-sensitive positions — including, but
       not limited to, electricians, HVAC technicians, refrigera-
       tion technicians, equipment operators, drivers, field
       supervisors, and any employee performing work at heights,
       on energized electrical systems, or on client project
       sites — shall be subject to unannounced random drug
       testing throughout the calendar year. Selection shall be
       made using a scientifically valid, neutral random selection
       method administered by an independent third-party
       administrator.

  4.3  Reasonable-Suspicion Testing
       An employee shall be required to submit to drug and/or
       alcohol testing when the Company has reasonable suspicion,
       based on specific, contemporaneous, articulable observa-
       tions concerning the appearance, behavior, speech, or body
       odors of the employee, that the employee is under the
       influence of drugs or alcohol. Reasonable-suspicion deter-
       minations shall be made by a trained supervisor and
       documented in writing.

  4.4  Post-Incident / Post-Accident Testing
       Any employee involved in a workplace accident, injury,
       near-miss, or property damage event shall be required to
       submit to drug and alcohol testing as soon as practicable,
       and in any event within thirty-two (32) hours of the event,
       where the employee's conduct could have contributed to the
       incident.

  4.5  Return-to-Duty and Follow-Up Testing
       An employee returning to work following a positive test
       result or completion of a substance abuse rehabilitation
       program (where return to work is offered at the Company's
       sole discretion) must pass a return-to-duty test and shall
       be subject to follow-up testing for a period of up to
       sixty (60) months.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. CONSEQUENCES OF VIOLATION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Any violation of this Policy will result in disciplinary action,
up to and including immediate termination of employment.
Specifically:

  (a) A confirmed positive drug or alcohol test result;
  (b) Refusal to submit to required testing;
  (c) Tampering with or adulterating a specimen; or
  (d) Possession, sale, or distribution of illegal substances
      on Company or project premises

shall result in immediate termination, subject to applicable law.

Applicants who fail or refuse a pre-employment test will have
their conditional offer of employment withdrawn and shall not be
eligible for re-application for a period of twelve (12) months.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. EMPLOYEE ASSISTANCE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Oak Shield encourages employees who recognize a substance abuse
problem to seek confidential assistance voluntarily, before any
violation of this Policy occurs. Employees may contact the Policy
Administrator for information regarding available community
resources, including SAMHSA's National Helpline (1-800-662-HELP).
Voluntary self-referral made prior to any incident, request for
testing, or notice of disciplinary action will not, in itself,
result in disciplinary action; however, completion of an appro-
priate treatment program may be required as a condition of
continued employment.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. CONFIDENTIALITY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

All information, interviews, reports, statements, memoranda, and
test results received by the Company through its drug-testing
program are confidential communications and shall not be released
except (a) to the tested employee upon request, (b) to Company
personnel on a strict need-to-know basis, (c) as required by law,
court order, or governmental agency, or (d) as required by a
prequalification platform or contractual obligation, with employee
identifying information redacted where permitted.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8. NOTIFICATION OF CRIMINAL CONVICTION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Consistent with the Drug-Free Workplace Act of 1988 (41 U.S.C.
§ 8101 et seq.), any employee who is convicted of any criminal
drug statute violation occurring in the workplace must notify the
Company in writing within five (5) calendar days of such convic-
tion. The Company will, in turn, notify any federal contracting
agency within ten (10) calendar days where required.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9. SEARCHES
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The Company reserves the right to conduct reasonable searches of
Company premises, lockers, desks, vehicles, equipment, and
personal property brought onto Company or project premises, where
the Company has reasonable suspicion of a Policy violation.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
10. SUBCONTRACTORS AND VISITORS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

All subcontractors performing work on Oak Shield project sites
shall maintain a drug-free workplace program substantially
equivalent to this Policy, and shall require their personnel to
comply with all client and project-site substance abuse require-
ments. Subcontractor non-compliance may result in immediate
removal from the project site and termination of the subcontract.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
11. POLICY ADMINISTRATION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The Policy Administrator is responsible for:

  (a) Communicating this Policy to all employees and new hires;
  (b) Conducting supervisor reasonable-suspicion training;
  (c) Maintaining all required records, including testing logs
      and signed Acknowledgment Forms, for a period of not less
      than five (5) years;
  (d) Coordinating with the third-party administrator and SAMHSA-
      certified laboratory; and
  (e) Reviewing this Policy at least annually for compliance with
      applicable law and client requirements.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
12. COMPLIANCE WITH APPLICABLE LAW
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

This Policy is intended to comply with all applicable federal,
Texas, and local law, including but not limited to the Drug-Free
Workplace Act of 1988, the Americans with Disabilities Act, and
the Texas Workforce Commission's voluntary drug-free workplace
guidelines. In the event of any conflict between this Policy and
applicable law, applicable law shall control.

This Policy does not create an employment contract or alter the
at-will employment relationship between Oak Shield Service LLC
and its employees.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
13. APPROVAL
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────



    ________________________________            ___________________
    Xinchao Xi, President                       Date
    Oak Shield Service LLC



═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                       END OF POLICY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

## 关键注意点

1. **不要写"zero tolerance for medical marijuana"**。德州现在没有合法娱乐用大麻，但医用 CBD 已合法。政策口径是"impairment on the job + positive test = termination"，回避大麻是否"合法"的辩论。
2. **不要承诺 EAP（员工援助计划）**。Oak Shield 规模不够买 EAP，§6 只说"提供资源信息 + SAMHSA 热线"即可，不要承诺公司付治疗费。
3. **Post-Incident 测试 32 小时窗口**是 OSHA 2016 年指导意见的边界，超过这个时间药物代谢完测了也没意义。
4. **签字版必须扫描存档**。ISN 审核员有时会要求看签字页扫描件 — 给Xinchao原件 + 一份扫描 PDF。
5. **Acknowledgment Form 要每年重签一次**（或政策修订时重签）。ISN 抽查时会问"你们多久重签一次"。
